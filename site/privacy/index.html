Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ReminisLink is an iPhone app that manages the games of your retro handheld over your local Wi-Fi network. It is built so that your data never leaves your network, except the copies of your saves that you keep in your own iCloud Drive.

What we collect

Nothing. ReminisLink has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The developer receives no data about you or about how you use the app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Pairing keys of your handhelds, in the iOS Keychain, readable only by ReminisLink and its share extension, on this device only (not synced with iCloud).
  • The names and preferences of your paired handhelds, in the storage of the app.
  • A cache of box art shown in the library, which iOS may clear at any time.
  • Temporary copies of the files you send (from Files or the share sheet), kept only until the transfer ends.

Deleting the app removes all of it (but not the copies of your saves in iCloud Drive, see below). Unpairing a handheld removes its key.

What goes to your handheld

ReminisLink talks only to the handhelds you paired, on your local network (found with Bonjour, then over HTTP on that network). Every request is signed with the pairing key.

  • The files you choose to send (games, saves, patches, cheat files).
  • When pairing, a name for your iPhone (the one iOS gives apps, usually “iPhone”), shown in the list of paired devices of the handheld. You can remove it there at any time.
  • Links you type or share: the app sends the link to your handheld, and the handheld downloads the file itself, from its own connection. The site you link to sees the handheld, not the app.

Copies of your saves (iCloud Drive)

ReminisLink can copy the saves of your handhelds (with their clock files) to your iPhone: with the Sync button of a handheld, and automatically when it connects (you can turn this off in the settings of the app). Up to 10 versions of each save are kept.

The copies go to your own iCloud Drive, in the ReminisLink folder of the Files app, where you can see, move or delete them. iCloud is Apple’s service, under your Apple account and Apple’s privacy policy: the developer has no server and never receives these copies. If iCloud Drive is off, the copies stay on your iPhone (Files > On My iPhone > ReminisLink) and are deleted with the app.

Permissions

  • Local network: to find your handheld and talk to it.
  • Camera: only to scan the QR code shown by your handheld. No picture is taken or saved.

Children

ReminisLink collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

reminis.link uses no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with a new date.
