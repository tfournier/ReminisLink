Your iPhone, companion of your retro handheld

Open ReminisLink on the handheld, manage your games, saves, save states, cheats, patches and apps from your phone over your home Wi-Fi, close it and play. No cable, no SD card to take out, no account.

Two parts that work together

ReminisLink is an iPhone app and a free tool for the handheld. They talk to each other only over your local network.

  • The app for iPhone

    Finds your handheld on the Wi-Fi network, pairs with a QR code and shows your whole library: systems, games, saves, save states and cheats.

  • The tool on the handheld

    A free pak for NextUI, shown as “ReminisLink” in the Tools menu. Open it when you want to manage your games; it only runs while no game is running.

Bring your own files

ReminisLink does not provide games, has no catalog to download from and does not suggest where to find any. You add the games, saves, patches and cheat files you already own: from the Files app or from the share sheet of any app.

Features

  • Library

    The games found on the SD card in one list, including the ones you copied by hand, with search and filters by system. Size, save, save states, cheats and box art for each game: tap the box art to change it, and download any file of a game to your iPhone.

  • Saves

    Download a save to your iPhone, replace it from Files, make named backup copies and restore them in one tap.

  • Save sync

    Copies of your saves on your iPhone or in your own iCloud Drive, made each time you connect, with up to 10 versions of each save to restore.

  • Save states

    See every slot with its thumbnail. Lock a state: if a game overwrites it, ReminisLink puts it back the next time it opens.

  • Cheats

    Turn cheats on or off, edit, reorder and delete them. Import cheat files (.cht) from your iPhone or the SD card.

  • Patches

    Apply IPS, BPS and UPS patches (translations, fixes, improvements) to a game you own. The result goes to a new file by default.

  • Apps

    Install tool and emulator paks from Files or the SD card, replace or remove them. Browse the SD card, read only.

  • Always up to date

    The app refreshes by itself when something changes on the handheld, even from another device, and goes back to its list as soon as you close ReminisLink.

  • The whole family

    Pair as many iPhones as you like. Devices on the same Apple Account share one pairing through iCloud Keychain. Changes run one at a time.

  • Nothing is lost

    Deleted or replaced files go to the trash of the handheld, until they reach 30 days of counted age (purged at launch) or you empty it. A save is always copied before it is replaced or restored.

  • Private and secure

    No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Encrypted connection to the handheld on your local network, recognized by the public-key fingerprint in its QR code. Apart from the presence check and pairing, requests are signed with a key kept in your Keychain; of your devices, the SD card only keeps public keys.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Install the pak

    Download ReminisLink.pak.zip from the releases page and unzip it into /Tools/tg5040/ on the SD card of the handheld.

  2. Open it

    Turn on Wi-Fi in the NextUI settings, then open Tools > ReminisLink. Your iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

  3. Scan the QR code

    In the iPhone app, tap Add a Console, scan the QR code shown on the handheld, then tap Open the Console. Done: manage your games, then press B on the handheld and play.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later (iPad and Mac are not supported for now).
  • A TrimUI Brick running NextUI, with the free ReminisLink pak. More handhelds are planned.
  • The iPhone and the handheld on the same Wi-Fi network.
