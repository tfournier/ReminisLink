Your iPhone, companion of your retro handheld

Open ReminisLink on the handheld, manage your games, saves, save states, cheats and patches from your phone over your home Wi-Fi, close it and play. No cable, no SD card to take out, no account.

Coming soon on the App Store Source code on GitHub

Two parts that work together

ReminisLink is an iPhone app and a free tool for the handheld. Everything stays on your local network.

  • The app for iPhone

    Finds your handheld on the Wi-Fi network, pairs with a QR code and shows your whole library: systems, games, saves, save states and cheats.

  • The tool on the handheld

    A free pak for NextUI, shown as “ReminisLink” in the Tools menu. Open it when you want to manage your games; it only runs while no game is running.

Bring your own files

ReminisLink does not provide, list or suggest any game. You add the games, saves, patches and cheat files you already own: from the Files app, from the share sheet of any app, or from a link that the handheld downloads itself.

Features

  • Library

    Every system and game on the SD card, including the ones you copied by hand, with size, save, save states, cheats and box art. Search and sort.

  • Saves

    Download a save to your iPhone, replace it from Files, make named backup copies and restore them in one tap.

  • Save states

    See every slot with its thumbnail. Lock a state: if a game overwrites it, ReminisLink puts it back the next time it opens.

  • Cheats

    Turn cheats on or off, edit, reorder and delete them. Import cheat files (.cht) from your iPhone, a link or the SD card.

  • Patches

    Apply IPS, BPS and UPS patches (translations, fixes, improvements) to a game you own. The result goes to a new file by default.

  • Nothing is lost

    Deleted or replaced files go to the trash of the handheld for at least 30 days. A save is always copied before it is replaced or restored.

  • Private

    No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Requests are signed with a key kept in the iPhone Keychain.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Install the pak

    On the handheld, open Pak Store and install ReminisLink. Or unzip the latest release into /Tools/tg5040/ on the SD card.

  2. Open it

    Turn on Wi-Fi in the NextUI settings, then open Tools > ReminisLink. Your iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

  3. Scan the QR code

    In the iPhone app, scan the QR code shown on the handheld (or type its 6-digit code). Done: manage your games, then press B on the handheld and play.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • A TrimUI Brick running NextUI, with the free ReminisLink pak. More handhelds are planned.
  • The iPhone and the handheld on the same Wi-Fi network.
