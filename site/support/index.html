Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Frequently asked questions

New to ReminisLink? Start with the installation guide.

The app does not find my handheld
  • Is ReminisLink open on the handheld? Turn on Wi-Fi in the NextUI settings, then open Tools > ReminisLink. The pak only runs while no game is running, and the app can only see the handheld while it is open. If the handheld went to sleep, wake it up.
  • Same Wi-Fi network. The iPhone and the handheld must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Mobile data does not work.
  • No guest network. Guest networks, and routers with “client isolation” or “AP isolation” turned on, stop devices from seeing each other. Use your main network, or turn that option off in the router settings.
  • VPN. Some VPNs and network filters on the iPhone block the local network. Turn them off while you use ReminisLink.
  • Local network access. If you declined the request the first time, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network on the iPhone and turn on ReminisLink.
  • Still nothing? Close ReminisLink on the handheld with B, open it again, then try again in the app.
How do I pair my iPhone?
  • Open ReminisLink on the handheld. The first time, it shows a QR code; once a device is paired, open Settings > Pair a New Device to show it. In the iPhone app, tap Add a Console and scan that code.
  • Pairing uses the QR code only: there is no code to type. Each QR code is valid 10 minutes, and pairing is open only while it is shown.
  • If the scanner does not open, allow the camera in Settings > ReminisLink > Camera, or scan the QR code with the Camera app of the iPhone.
  • After too many failed attempts, the handheld blocks pairing for a short time and then shows a new QR code.

Step by step: installation guide.

Can I use several iPhones, or share the handheld with my family?

Yes. Your Apple devices signed in with the same Apple Account share one pairing through iCloud Keychain: pair once, from any of them. A device with another Apple Account pairs on its own: on the handheld, open Settings > Pair a New Device and scan the QR code with that device.

All paired devices can use the handheld at the same time. Changes run one at a time (see “The console is busy” below).

Does it work on iPad or Mac?

Not officially for now: ReminisLink is an iPhone app, and it is only tested on iPhone. An iPad or a Mac may still be able to open iPhone apps, but nothing is guaranteed there. iPad and Mac support is planned as a separate step.

How do I remove a paired device?
  • From the app: open the handheld, then Console > Unpair. The handheld forgets the pairing, and it is removed from all your Apple devices that share it.
  • From the handheld: open Settings > Devices and select the device. Forget removes its pairing (it will have to pair again). Disconnect only refuses its requests until it reconnects from the app; closing ReminisLink also lifts it.
  • If you lend or lose the SD card, or sell the handheld, use Settings > Forget all devices, then pair again the devices you keep.
Why do I have to pair again after an update?

When a version changes how devices pair or sign their requests, the pak deletes the old pairings when it first starts. This happened when ReminisLink moved to encrypted connections with one key per device. The app then shows Pair Again next to the handheld: tap it and scan a new QR code (Settings > Pair a New Device on the handheld). The release notes say when this is needed.

The app also asks you to pair again if the handheld no longer has the identity it had when you paired (for example after its ReminisLink data folder was deleted from the SD card). In that case nothing is sent to it until you scan its QR code again.

Is the connection secure?
  • Encrypted. The app and the handheld talk over HTTPS, on your local network only. ReminisLink on the handheld never connects to the internet; only iCloud, if you use it, goes through the internet.
  • The right handheld. The handheld has its own certificate. The QR code you scan contains the fingerprint of its public key, and the app only accepts a certificate with that key, even when it pairs: another device on the network cannot pretend to be your handheld.
  • Only your devices. Each request is signed with a private key that stays in your Keychain. Only two requests are unsigned: checking that the handheld is there, and the pairing itself, which needs the one-time secret of the QR code. Of your devices, the SD card only keeps public keys: a copy of the card is not enough to control the handheld. It does hold the handheld’s own certificate key, so keep the card safe.
“The console is busy with another change”

The handheld makes changes (send, delete, replace, restore, patch, cheats…) one at a time, even when several devices are connected. If one waits more than 10 seconds for the previous one to finish, it is refused and nothing is changed. Wait a moment and try again.

Why did the app go back to the list of consoles?

The handheld tells the app as soon as ReminisLink closes on it (with B, for example): the app goes back to its list with a short message. Files you were sending from the iPhone are paused: resume them from Transfers while the app is still open. The handheld keeps their progress for 24 hours of inactivity, but the app does not keep its transfer list once it is closed. A patch or an installation that the handheld was running is stopped, and its partial file deleted: start it again later. If the handheld goes to sleep or out of Wi-Fi range without closing ReminisLink, the app shows a lost connection instead. While the handheld is open, the app refreshes what it shows by itself when something changes, even from another device.

Are my saves copied to my iPhone or to iCloud?

Yes, if you want. The app copies the saves of your handheld (with their clock files): with Sync Saves in the Console tab, and automatically each time it connects (you can turn this off in the settings of the app). It keeps up to 10 versions of each save, and you can restore one to the handheld; the handheld first keeps a copy of its current save.

With iCloud Drive turned on, the copies go to the ReminisLink folder of your own iCloud Drive (Files app). Otherwise they stay on the iPhone (Files > On My iPhone > ReminisLink). Save states are not copied.

Can I install or remove apps (paks) on the handheld?

Yes. In the Console tab, open Apps: it lists the tool and emulator paks of the handheld. You can install one from the Files app of the iPhone (a zip archive or a .pak folder), or from a file already on the SD card. A pak of the same name is only replaced after you confirm, and the old one goes to the trash. Removing a pak also moves it to the trash. ReminisLink cannot remove or replace itself.

ReminisLink does not check what a pak does: install only paks you trust.

Tap Storage in the Console tab to browse the SD card. This browser is read only: you can look at folders and download a file to your iPhone.

Where do deleted files go? (the trash)

ReminisLink never erases your files directly. A deleted game, a game patched in place, a replaced save state, an older cheat file, a removed game image or a removed or replaced pak goes to the trash of the handheld, on the SD card, where it stays until it reaches 30 days of counted age (purged at launch) or you empty the trash. Replacing or restoring a save also makes a backup copy first.

The Trash tab of the app lists what is in it, with its size and when it will be deleted. The space is freed only when entries are deleted: from that tab (one entry, or Empty Trash), which cannot be undone, or automatically. Entries are purged at launch once they reach 30 days of counted age. The clock of the handheld is not reliable, so it counts the time while ReminisLink runs, plus at most 7 days for each period in between: an entry can stay longer than 30 calendar days.

The app cannot put a file back yet. To get a file back by hand, look in the folder /.userdata/shared/ReminisLink/trash/ of the SD card: each entry keeps the files under their original path.

“This game uses a NextUI setting that ReminisLink does not handle yet”

NextUI can store saves and save states in several formats, chosen in its settings. ReminisLink 1.0 handles the default formats only. With another format, or when NextUI names saves after the file inside a zip archive, ReminisLink refuses to change the saves and save states of the games concerned and changes nothing. Games, cheats and patches still work.

Changing that setting in NextUI changes where your saves are looked for: keep it if you rely on it. Support for the other formats is planned.

Which handhelds are supported?

Version 1.0 works with the TrimUI Brick running NextUI. Other handhelds and systems are planned. The pak needs Wi-Fi, and the iPhone app needs iOS 17 or later.

Where do I get games?

ReminisLink lists the games on your SD card, but it does not provide games, has no catalog to download from and does not suggest where to find any. Bring your own files: games, saves, patches and cheat files you already own, from the Files app or the share sheet of another app. A file from the web is downloaded on your iPhone first (it goes to the Files app), then added: the handheld never downloads anything itself.

How do I install, update or uninstall the pak?

Download ReminisLink.pak.zip from the releases page and unzip it into /Tools/tg5040/ on the SD card. To update, replace the ReminisLink.pak folder with the new one. The installation guide has every step, including uninstalling and where ReminisLink keeps its data.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/tfournier/ReminisLink/issues. A free GitHub account is needed. You can write in English or French.

To help us, please give:

  • the version of the iPhone app, and the version of the pak (shown on the handheld in Settings > Version and log);
  • your handheld and your NextUI version;
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened;
  • if possible, the log file of the pak: /.userdata/tg5040/logs/ReminisLink.txt on the SD card. Read it before you share it: it may contain the names of your handheld, of your devices and of your files, the identifiers of your devices and their addresses on your local network.

Never share the folder /.userdata/shared/ReminisLink/tls/: it holds the private key of the certificate of your handheld. (clients.json holds, for each paired device, its identifier, name, platform, the types of device seen, its public key and the pairing date.)
