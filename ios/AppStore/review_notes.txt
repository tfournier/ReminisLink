ReminisLink is a companion app for a retro handheld game console (TrimUI Brick running the NextUI firmware). It only works with a handheld on the same Wi-Fi network that runs the free ReminisLink tool; it has no account, no server and no in-app purchase.

The app does not provide, list or link to any game. Users add files they own, from the Files app or the share sheet. Neither the app nor the handheld downloads files from the internet.

Because the app needs the handheld, a video of the whole flow (pairing with the QR code, library, adding a file, saves, states, cheats, patch) is attached: <LINK TO THE VIDEO>.

Permissions: local network (to find the handheld with Bonjour and talk to it over HTTPS on the local network; the app only accepts the handheld's certificate whose fingerprint is in the pairing QR code) and camera (to scan the pairing QR code shown by the handheld).
